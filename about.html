About

Strategy into practical action.

PRAXAK is an India-based studio making high-retention short-form video and content systems for coaches, founders, podcasters and service businesses worldwide.

We don't promise views, followers or sales. We promise consistent, on-time, retention-first output.

Process

Three steps. Zero editing on your side.

Drop the raw files

Footage, podcast exports or links go into a private shared Drive folder. Our one-page filming guide keeps the batch usable.

Day 0 · your evening

Studio edit and QC

Hook scripted, cut assembled, captions, B-roll, SFX and motion graphics added, then a frame-by-frame check before delivery.

Overnight · IST

Review and post

Leave timestamped notes, use your revision round, approve. Then we lock a monthly cadence so the output compounds.

On time, every time
