Questions

Before you ask

How do I send footage?

Footage, podcast exports or links go into a private shared Drive folder. Our one-page filming guide keeps each batch usable.

How fast is delivery?

Every batch is turned around within 48 hours. We edit overnight in India, so files you drop in your evening are already in progress while you sleep.

How many revisions do I get?

Revision rounds are fixed per plan, two at most. Leave timestamped notes on the draft and we apply them in your round.

Is there a contract?

No lock-in. Retainers run month to month and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

How does payment work?

50% upfront before work begins and the balance on delivery. Ad spend, premium stock and third-party tools are billed separately.

Do you guarantee views?

No. We don't promise views, followers or sales. We promise consistent, on-time, retention-first output.

Judge us on your own footage.

Send one published video or podcast episode. We cut a ~40-second sample so you can see the edit before paying for anything.
